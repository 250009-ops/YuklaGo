# Languages and copy

Locales: Uzbek Latin and Russian. Client screens ship both. The staff console is Uzbek only.

Strings live in `packages/i18n/locales/uz.json` and `ru.json`. The screen hand-off is the source for the exact lines. Do not paraphrase a must-show line.

## Orthography

- oʻ, gʻ, Oʻ, Gʻ use U+02BB. The tutuq belgisi (maʼlumot) uses U+02BC.
- If the user types o, O, g, or G followed by `'`, `‘`, `’`, `ʼ`, or a backtick, replace that pair with the letter plus U+02BB. The pattern is `/([oOgG])['\u2018\u2019\u02BC`]/g` → `"$1\u02BB"` (AC-42.2).
- Search folds those variants to U+02BB before compare (AC-42.3).
- UI font is Onest. Never load IBM Plex Sans (AC-42.4). Codes, grafa ids, and URLs use JetBrains Mono. Money uses tabular figures, not mono, except the cabinet placeholder the hand-off asks to set in JetBrains Mono.

## Must-show lines

They sit on the screen, not only behind an info control, and they do not count toward the 60-word body cap. The list is `packages/i18n/must-show.json`, taken from the screen hand-off §5.1. A review test checks that the locale files contain each Uzbek line and each Russian line.

## Banned phrases

`packages/i18n/banned-phrases.json`. The checker `packages/i18n/check-copy.mjs` normalises apostrophes and fails if a banned phrase appears under `apps/`, `packages/ui/`, or `packages/i18n/locales/`.

Banned on every surface:

|---|---|
| broker oʻrnini bosadi | заменяет брокера |
| rasmiylashtirib beramiz | оформим за вас |
| bojxona tasdiqlagan | одобрено таможней |
| rasmiy hamkor | официальный партнёр |
| 100% kafolat | 100% гарантия |

Spec §10 says the Russian forms of the last three were not given by Huquq and are [PENDING: Huquq]. The screen hand-off still forbids those Russian strings. The lint follows the hand-off so they cannot ship. Do not describe the Russian lines as a legal opinion.

Also do not write, even though they are not all in the automatic list: "brokerni toʻliq almashtiradi", "biz siz uchun topshiramiz", "ed2 bilan integratsiyalangan", "xatosiz", "jarimasiz", "bir tugmada topshiriladi", "NCTS" (say "E-Tranzit"), "Vakolatli mutaxassis", "imzolovchilar roʻyxati". Do not use "Tasdiqlangan" for something the user confirmed. Use "Siz tasdiqladingiz". Do not use "Разрешён" for the import status. Use "Запрета нет" / "Mumkin". Do not use "NO-GO". Use "Taqiqlangan".

"Rasmiy" is reserved for "Rasmiy stavka topilmadi", "Rasmiy sayt · YuklaGo emas", and "rasmiy kod emas" inside the disclaimer "YuklaGo izohi, rasmiy kod emas". A duty rate the specialist has signed may use the chip "Rasmiy" with source and date (PE-21). The code chip on C-10 says "TIF TN · 18.11.2026", not "Rasmiy roʻyxat".

## Public line

"Brokersiz, oʻzingiz topshirasiz — tez va tushunarli".

Russian: "Без брокера — подаёте сами, быстро и понятно".

## Layout limits from the hand-off

One filled button per screen. At most two visible secondary actions. Headline at most 6 words. No sentence over 14 words. Body at most 60 words, must-show lines excluded. Article numbers appear as chips, not inside sentences. These are UI review rules. The copy lint does not count words. Reviewers do, against the Figma frames and the locale files.

The Russian labels in `ru.json` are the five heads of the C-14 Russian stage line in the screen hand-off: Документы, Проверка, Просмотр, Заполнение, Статус. Do not invent a sixth stage.
